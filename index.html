FEATURED UX CASE STUDY · THE YARD STUDIO

A shared design workspace built around the Howard community.

Overview

The Yard Studio is a concept for a shared digital environment where Howard students, student organizations, and departments can collaborate on the digital experiences that shape campus life.

Instead of generic folders and workspaces, the product borrows the mental model of campus itself. The Yard becomes an open project hub, Founders Library becomes the shared design system, and Blackburn becomes a space for critique and discussion.

Problem statement

Howard students constantly create digital experiences for events, clubs, elections, publications, and organizations — but the work is often scattered across individual accounts, group chats, and isolated files.

This makes it harder to discover active projects, reuse trusted resources, bring in collaborators, and preserve what previous teams already learned.

How might we create a shared design environment where Howard students can discover projects, collaborate across organizations, reuse campus resources, and contribute their skills?
